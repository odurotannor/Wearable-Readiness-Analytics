Wearable Health Readiness Analytics

What daily habits should a wearable app nudge to improve next-day readiness? A Tableau analysis of 7 weeks of Fitbit data.

View on Tableau Public GitHub repo

Key findings

Late bedtimes cut sleepBedtimes after 2 AM pushed sleep below 6 hours (r ≈ −0.59 between bedtime and hours asleep).
Under 6 hours hurts readinessReadiness dropped on nights under 6 hours; beyond that, extra sleep added little.
Steps don't predict readinessHitting a step goal had no consistent effect on next-day readiness at any goal level.

Recommendation: nudge earlier bedtimes rather than higher step goals.

How it was built

  • Data: Google Health / Fitbit export, Sep 15 – Nov 2, 2026 (heart rate, steps, sleep, readiness, exercise).
  • Preparation (Python): converted UTC to local time across time zones and DST, resampled heart rate and steps to 5-minute intervals, removed duplicate sleep records and placeholder zeros.
  • Data model: star schema with 4 fact tables (5-minute activity, daily, sleep, exercise) and shared Date, Hour, Location and Activity Type dimensions, related in Tableau.
  • Tableau techniques: FIXED LOD expressions, LOOKUP table calculations, a step-goal parameter, a heatmap, scatter plots with trend lines, a map, dashboard actions and a .hyper extract.

Limitations

  • Single-user case study covering 49 days, so the findings describe one person, not users in general.
  • Readiness was recorded on 29 of 49 days; some location results rest on 1–2 days.
  • Locations are shown at city level only, for privacy.
